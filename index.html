Quantum Chess

Like normal chess, but you have the ability to quantumly superimpose your pieces, splitting into a real and fake version of the piece. Your opponent cannot see which option is real or fake. Like Schrödinger's cat, you can only find out which of the options is real when you interact with the system (take either of the pieces). To win the game, you must capture the king; there are no check or checkmate conditions in this game.

Originally coded in Python, then translated to JavaScript for compatibility with static website hosting (this portfolio website). Click to view.
